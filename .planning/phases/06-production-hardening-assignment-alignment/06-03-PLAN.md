<objective>
Expose `/healthz` and `/readyz` on the MQ control-plane ServeMux (:8080) so Kubernetes can detect a
live-but-wedged or shutting-down broker (OBS-01, finding F-02). Readiness reflects gRPC serving
state via a new lock-free `MQServer.IsShuttingDown()`.

Purpose: a single-replica in-memory broker with no readiness signal is invisible to Kubernetes — a
wedged MQ keeps receiving traffic. Health endpoints close that gap without touching delivery
semantics (ADR-001 untouched).
Output: `mqhttp.HealthzHandler`, `mqhttp.ReadyzHandler`, `MQServer.IsShuttingDown`, mux registration.

## Phase Goal (MVP framing)

**As an** operator, **I want to** probe the MQ's liveness and readiness over HTTP, **so that**
Kubernetes stops routing to a shutting-down or wedged broker. After Task 2 a real probe against
:8080/readyz flips to 503 the moment the broker begins shutdown.
</objective>
