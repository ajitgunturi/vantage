<objective>
Wire Kubernetes-native liveness/readiness probes (OPS-07, F-03), resource requests/limits (OPS-08,
F-04), and an optional gateway HPA plus a documented scaling story (OPS-09, F-05) into the Helm
sub-charts — pointing probes at the OBS-01 health endpoints added in Plans 02–05.

Purpose: BestEffort pods with no probes are first evicted and invisible to Kubernetes health
tracking; HPA-by-CPU is impossible without requests. This makes the deployment "stable and elastic"
as the assignment frames it, without touching the single-replica MQ invariant.
Output: probe + resources blocks in all four deployments, a values-gated HPA template, per-service
values defaults, streamer/collector named health ports.

## Phase Goal (MVP framing)

**As a** cluster operator, **I want** every service to declare probes and resource requests and the
gateway to optionally autoscale, **so that** Kubernetes can restart wedged pods, schedule under node
pressure, and scale the read tier under load. After Task 2 `helm template` renders probes+resources
on every pod; after Task 3 an enabled HPA renders for the gateway.
</objective>
