<objective>
Give the collector an HTTP health listener (`/healthz` + `/readyz`) on a new env-configurable port so
Kubernetes probes have a target (OBS-01, finding F-02). Expose readiness via a `Runner` wrapper
without breaking the `Consume`/`Run` seams the E2E and reconnect tests depend on.

Purpose: the collector exposes no HTTP surface today; Helm probes (Plan 06) need one, and readiness
should reflect whether the MQ Consume stream is actually connected.
Output: `collector.Runner` with `IsReady()`, `Config.HealthAddr` (COLLECTOR_HEALTH_ADDR, default
:9001), and a health goroutine in the collector main.

## Phase Goal (MVP framing)

**As an** operator, **I want to** probe the collector's health over HTTP, **so that** Kubernetes can
gate the pod on whether its MQ Consume stream is connected. After Task 2 a probe against
:9001/readyz flips to 200 once the stream opens and back toward not-ready across a disconnect.
</objective>
